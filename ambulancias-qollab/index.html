02 — Red-team the answer

The classical plan gets a vote.

Every method uses the same city cells, radius, fleet size and single-outage score. Exact search checks every valid combination. QAOA offers a sampled candidate. The device does not get to pick its own opponent.

Calculating the scenario…
WHAT THE CIRCUIT DOES A 2-layer QAOA circuit searches only combinations that contain exactly K units. Its cost phase scores the complete coverage function, including the outage case.WHAT THIS IS NOT No dispatch recommendation, patient outcome, quantum speedup, or measured drive time.

DATA / METHOD / LIMITS

01
Published events
Unique injury-crash records, point-in-polygon filtered to CABA census radios. A collision signal, not every medical emergency.
02
Explicit assumptions
Historical severity weights, representative candidate points and straight-line radii are visible modeling choices, not official policy.
03
Classical accountability
Greedy, greedy plus one-site swaps and exact enumeration run on the same objective. QAOA's ideal result is a local statevector prediction.
04
Useful next dataset
Operational call locations, dispatch logs, verified base/depot lots, road-network travel times, and unit availability by hour.

      

      
A product hypothesis, not a sales claim
Plan capacity.
Inspect the failure mode.
First user to interview: an emergency-response operations planner. Smallest credible pilot: one historical call archive, approved candidate lots, actual road travel times, two operating shifts, a held-out validation period. We would compare missed-demand weight and worst-neighborhood travel time against current positioning and integer optimization. Nothing in this demo has been validated with SAME, a municipality, or patients.
